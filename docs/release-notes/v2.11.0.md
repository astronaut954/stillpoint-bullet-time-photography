- One process per camera: each Canon camera now runs in its own helper process (StillpointWorker). Cameras connect in parallel, shoot and download at the same time, and if one camera freezes the camera software only that camera is affected: the app restarts its process and the camera comes back with the same name. Per-camera logs in the app's Logs folder. Tested on Mac with SL3, T7 and T5
- Timing: "One after another" and "Sweep" are now one mode, Sweep, with a free interval from 0.05 s and shortcuts (0.1, 0.2, 0.3, 0.4 s); Direction (1 → N, N → 1, back and forth) is available in every mode, including Groups, Odd / even and Custom (over the steps). Custom steps can be reordered by dragging
- Cameras need about 1.2 s between their own shots (measured on the T7: a photo asked sooner is refused). The timeline now warns when a plan repeats a camera too soon, the shoot button asks before shooting such a plan ("Fix timing" / "Shoot anyway"), and a refused photo is shown while shooting, summarized at the end ("Done with 1 photo missing: …") and marked on the session ("1 missing")
- Downloads no longer delay a camera's next shot: while a plan still has shots to fire, the camera keeps its photos and the app downloads them in order after the last shot (the T7 and SL3 held 6 RAW photos; the older T5 holds only 3–4 and refuses further shots in longer plans)
- Simulated cameras (STILLPOINT_FAKE_CAMERAS) refuse too-soon repeats like a real camera, so the warnings can be tried without cameras
- Interface: timeline rows evenly spaced around the dashed line; dragged cameras, steps and thumbnails are translucent; no text gets selected while dragging; top bar buttons aligned with the panels; the window opens a little taller so the Bullet time panel does not scroll
- On Windows this version compiles and was tested with simulated cameras; the Live window and real cameras on Windows are still untested
