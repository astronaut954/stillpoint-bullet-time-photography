- Camera settings reorganized: the table shows what defines the capture (ISO, Shutter, Aperture, Exp. comp., Metering, Format, Ratio) and the ± button opens a Color panel with white balance, WB fine-tune, Picture Style and its adjustments
- New Ratio setting (3:2, 4:3, 16:9, 1:1) on each camera or on all; cameras that cannot change it over USB show it as fixed, and the change goes to the others
- Match automatically now works in three steps: choose what to match, take the test shots, then review the result (a table of changes per camera and before/after photos with a draggable split, side by side or flip, and play) before anything changes on the cameras.
