- Edit in Lightroom Classic: Export sessions has a new step, "2 Edit", that sends the photos of a session to Adobe Lightroom Classic and brings the edited photos back
- Stillpoint installs its own Lightroom plugin and keeps one Lightroom catalog per project, created from an empty catalog you make once
- Inside Lightroom, the Stillpoint panel and menu have "Export to Stillpoint", "Apply photo 01 edit", "Go to session" and "Cancel editing"
- Edited photos show in the previews and are used in the exports; "Original / Edited" switches between them and "Revert to original" removes the edits (⌘Z brings them back)
- The framing chosen in Stillpoint goes to Lightroom as the crop of each photo; a crop moved, resized or turned in Lightroom comes back as the alignment of that photo
- Lightroom does not send photos back when the crop was changed to another aspect ratio; "Put back the Stillpoint crop" fixes it
- The framing of a session is locked while it is in Lightroom or has edited photos, so the edits always match the crop
- Aspect ratio can be horizontal or vertical: two buttons next to the ratios turn 3:2, 4:3 and 16:9 into 2:3, 3:4 and 9:16
- Export sessions now has four steps: Sessions, Edit, Outputs, Review
- Export sessions remembers which sessions were ticked after you close Stillpoint; new sessions come ticked
- Undo and redo in "Align photos" no longer skip or lose steps
- Label several sessions at once: right-click with several sessions selected
- Project window: "Edited only" ticks the sessions with any edited photo
