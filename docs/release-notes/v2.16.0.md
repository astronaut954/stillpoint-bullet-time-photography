- Camera settings reorganized: the table shows what defines the capture (ISO, Shutter, Aperture, Exp. comp., Metering, Format, Ratio) and the ± button opens a Color panel with white balance, WB fine-tune, Picture Style and its adjustments
- New Ratio setting (3:2, 4:3, 16:9, 1:1) on each camera or on all; cameras that cannot change it over USB show it as fixed, and the change goes to the others
- Match automatically now works in three steps: choose what to match, take the test shots, then review the result (a table of changes per camera and before/after photos with a draggable split, side by side or flip, and play) before anything changes on the cameras. Apply to the cameras you tick; one Undo brings them all back
- The app measures the real shape of each photo: a camera whose test shot comes out in another ratio than it reports gets an amber mark and a warning icon in Camera settings; a session shot with mixed ratios gets a "Mixed ratios" tag (list, header, thumbnails) and, the first time, a window to open Camera settings or choose a ratio to crop to on export
- Export sessions now works in three steps, shown at the top: 1 Sessions (which sessions and the framing of each), 2 Outputs (Photos, Sequence, Lenticular) and 3 Review (a table of what will be exported, the name and the folder). Earlier steps open with one click; the green button moves forward once the step is ready. The window always opens on step 1 and asks before you leave
- Framing: an "Aspect ratio" (As shot, 3:2, 4:3, 16:9, 1:1) and a position for each session. The frame is the part every photo of the session covers, so no photo is enlarged; drag the preview to choose the part that stays. The originals are never changed
- Align photos: move, resize and rotate each photo over the previous and next ones (each with its own opacity), with handles on the preview, zoom, pan with the space bar, Fit to canvas and Reset. Photos stay locked to the frame, like in Lightroom. A session with mixed ratios asks for a ratio first; leave with "Done aligning" or Esc
- "Same framing" and "Same settings" boxes link the sessions (ticked), show when some differ (a dash, with a note) or let each change on its own (empty)
- Theater mode in Photos (with the thumbnails), Sequence and Lenticular
- Lenticular: "Placement" Fill (the paper shape wins and crops the framing, with a warning when they differ) or Fit (the whole framing with a black or white band; the color is set in Settings › Lenticular, with a link from Export)
- Mixed ratios: the choice made after a shoot applies to every session of that shoot and to the next shoots with the same cameras, until Camera settings change. The tag also shows in the Project window
- New "n deleted" tag on sessions with photos in the Trash
- Undo goes to where the change was made (step, session, tab, photo) and says where; it does nothing while a confirmation is open. The Lenticular band color is part of Undo
- Settings and About open above any window and close back to it
- Fixes: clicks on the session list right after a shoot no longer get lost; dragging a photo against the edge reaches the edge
