- Project: a folder of yours that keeps sessions without leaving the app (Originals/, Edited/, Exports/ and stillpoint-project.json). Open or create one from the project button in the top bar; new sessions go straight into the open project
- Sessions list: the project's sessions on top; the others under "Not in project" (collapsed). Hold and drag a session onto the project area or the project button to add it, onto "Not in project" to take it out
- Right-click a session: add or rename a label (with suggestions from the other labels), show it, open its folder (Originals and Edited in a project), add or remove from the project, delete
- Project window: rename the project in the name field at the top, labels per session, "Edited n / m" when Lightroom or Photoshop exports land in Edited/, Export… straight to Exports/ with "Use edited photos when available"; sessions stay after exporting
- Undo and redo (Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z; Cmd on macOS) for renaming the project, labels, add/remove from the project, camera order, photo order, camera settings and Copy from camera, and for deleting photos or sessions (they go to an app trash until Stillpoint is closed)
- Notices at the bottom: new look (UNDO / REDO / EVENT / WARNING), 2.5 s on screen, hover keeps them, X closes; every event is also written to Activity (camera-setting details only under Show technical details)
- Windows: Enter confirms and Esc cancels dialogs; clicking outside a window cancels it; selections and export settings are remembered; session times show seconds; the three panels share the same neon rail
- Not yet tested with real cameras in this version (session.json, .xmp alongside RAW, undo of camera settings, trash with RAW files) nor on Windows beyond compiling
