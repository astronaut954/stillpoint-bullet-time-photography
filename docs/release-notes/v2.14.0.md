- Lenticular print (first part): a new "Lenticular" choice in Save sessions / Export turns a session into one interlaced TIFF to print under a lenticular lens sheet. Pick the lens (40–100 LPI, or type yours), the printer's native dpi (600/720/1200/1440 or other), the print size (cm or inches, presets) and the effect (animation when tilting, or 3D); the window shows the columns per lens, dots per photo and warns when the combination cannot separate the photos. Drag the preview left and right to see the photos change. The TIFF carries the dpi so it prints at the exact size (print at 100%, never "fit to page")
